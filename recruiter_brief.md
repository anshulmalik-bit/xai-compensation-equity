# XAI Compensation Equity Audit

## The Problem
Companies know they have gender pay gaps, but they struggle to prove exactly how much of that gap is caused by legitimate differences (like tenure or role) versus structural bias. Without knowing the exact financial size and root cause of the discrimination, HR leaders cannot legally or effectively fix their payroll compliance.

## What Was Built
- An automated audit system that ingests employee HR data and mathematically separates fair pay differences from actual discrimination.
- A legally-aligned calculation engine that isolates the exact financial liability owed to underpaid female employees.
- A dual-interface reporting layer generating both a management web dashboard and a formal PDF compliance document for executive review.

## Key Finding
The audit successfully uncovered that the disparity was not just a generic penalty, but operated through a specific, fixable organizational mechanism. Subjective manager evaluation scores showed a **-0.275 mean penalty** for women. Identifying this precise pathway proves that the resulting **₹2.94 Lakh average penalty** per underpaid employee can be specifically and structurally addressed by recalibrating subjective manager performance reviews, rather than blindly adjusting base pay distributions.

## Regulatory Alignment
- **Equal Remuneration Act, 1976**: Tests and flags violations of the mandate for equal pay for equal work.
- **Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023**: Ensures localized, privacy-first processing of sensitive HR compensation data.
- **MeitY AI Guidelines**: Delivers fully transparent algorithmic explanations, preventing "black-box" discriminatory automated decision making.
